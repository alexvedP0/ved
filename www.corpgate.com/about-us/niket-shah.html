Niket Shah, Partner

Meet Niket, a pioneering entrepreneur whose innovative vision and impeccable business acumen have led to several successful businesses. He pursued his degree from the prestigious NM College of Commerce and further enhanced his expertise with an MBA specializing in finance from DSIMS. He brings valuable experience from his tenure at Viteos, one of the leading hedge fund firms, demonstrating his strong financial expertise.


As a serial entrepreneur, Niket has consistently demonstrated his ability to identify market needs and develop groundbreaking solutions. He launched Purec Hygiene, which introduced the world’s first automatic seat cover and specializes in manufacturing smart washroom essentials for those committed to a hygienic lifestyle.


Building on this success, he founded CORPGATE, a company dedicated to streamlining procurement processes for businesses, making them more efficient and cost-effective. In his role as a founder, Niket oversees procurement, operations, supply chain management, and sales. His dedicated focus on exports has successfully attracted numerous international clients, expanding the global reach of his ventures.


Niket's commitment to excellence and his innovative approach continue to drive the success and growth of his enterprises, making him a respected leader in the industry.
